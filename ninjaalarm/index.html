Ninja Alarm

A silent, vibration-only alarm for Apple Watch. Wake up without waking anyone else.

Getting Started

  1. Open Ninja Alarm on your Apple Watch and allow notifications (they are the backup buzz).
  2. Tap Add Alarm. Pick a time, or switch to Countdown for a nap.
  3. Choose a strength and a pattern, or tap out your own rhythm under Make your own.
  4. Tap Test in 1 minute, lower your wrist and feel it work with the screen off.
  5. Turn on Silent Mode on the watch so it only vibrates.

How it keeps ringing

Ninja Alarm vibrates until you press Stop. If you sleep through it, it switches to maximum strength after a few minutes. watchOS limits an alarm session to about 30 minutes; after that, Ninja Alarm keeps buzzing once a minute with notifications until you stop it.

Start softly: choose how long the alarm takes to grow from a whisper to full strength, from right away to 15 minutes.

Common Questions

Why do I need to open the app before an alarm?

watchOS only lets an app arm one alarm at a time, at most 36 hours ahead, and only while the app is open. Ninja Alarm re-arms the next alarm every time you stop one, so daily alarms keep going on their own. If an alarm is further away (for example over a weekend), you get a reminder the evening before: tap it and the alarm is armed.

The watch makes a sound when the alarm vibrates

Apple Watch plays a short sound with every vibration unless Silent Mode is on. Swipe up for Control Center and tap the bell to turn on Silent Mode. It stays on until you turn it off.

I don't feel the alarm

  • Pick a higher strength or the Drumroll pattern.
  • Wear the watch a little snug.
  • Make sure the watch is charged before bed.
  • Check that notifications are allowed in the Watch app on your iPhone.

System Requirements

Apple Watch: watchOS 10 or later. Ninja Alarm runs on the watch only; no iPhone app is needed.

Privacy

Ninja Alarm collects no data.

Your alarms and patterns are stored only on your Apple Watch. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Ninja Alarm does not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Track your usage or behaviour
  • Use analytics, advertising or tracking tools
  • Require an account
  • Share data with third parties

Permissions

Notifications: used only as a backup buzz if watchOS does not start the alarm, to keep buzzing after the 30-minute alarm session, and to remind you to arm an alarm that is more than 36 hours away.
