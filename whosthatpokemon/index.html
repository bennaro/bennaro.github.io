Who's That Pokémon?

A Guess Who-style game with Pokémon – play online against a friend. The app is in Norwegian.

How to Play

  1. Pick the Pokémon you want to be – or tap Random (Tilfeldig). Your opponent has to guess it.
  2. One player taps New game (Nytt spill), chooses the rules and sends the invitation.
  3. The other player opens the invitation link, or taps Join with code (Bli med med kode) and types in the code.
  4. Take turns asking yes/no questions and flip down the Pokémon that no longer fit.
  5. When you think you know the answer, press and hold a card and choose Guess (Gjett). A wrong guess loses the game!

Rules the Host Can Choose

Regions: Play with Pokémon from one or more regions (Kanto to Paldea), or all of them.

Number of cards: 16, 24, 32, 40 or 48 Pokémon on the board.

Own questions: Allow or disallow free-text questions. Ready-made questions (type, region, colour, height, weight, legendary …) are always answered automatically by the app, so nobody can lie. Free-text questions are answered by your opponent.

Common Issues

"You must be signed in to iCloud to play online"

The game uses iCloud to connect the two players. Open Settings → your name at the top, and sign in with your Apple ID.

"Could not find a game with that code"

  • Check that the code is typed correctly (letters and numbers, no spaces)
  • Make sure the other player has created the game and is waiting in the lobby

"This game already has two players"

Each game is for exactly two players. Create a new game to get a new code.

The pictures don't load

Pokémon pictures are downloaded from the internet the first time they are shown. Check your internet connection.

FAQ

What does it cost?
The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do both players need the app?
Yes. Both players need the app on their own iPhone and must be signed in to iCloud.

Can my opponent see my Pokémon?
No. Your Pokémon is only shown to your opponent when the round is over.

Is the colour question always right?
The colour comes from the official Pokédex, which lists one main colour per Pokémon. It may not always match what you would call it yourself.

Is this an official Pokémon app?
No. This is an unofficial fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc. or The Pokémon Company.
