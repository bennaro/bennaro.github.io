TxtMaker

Copy anything. Paste it as a Markdown, text, PDF or HTML file.

Coming soon to the Mac App StoreFree · macOS 13 or later

One click from clipboard to file

1. Copy

An AI's answer, part of a web page, a document, a screenshot.

2. Click

The TxtMaker icon in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥V in any app.

3. Paste

The file is in Downloads and on your clipboard. ⌘V attaches it in Slack, Teams, Mail or an AI chat.

Why

Many apps won't send or open .md files, and long text pasted into a chat gets cut or loses its formatting. TxtMaker gives you the format that works: PDF for people, Markdown or text for an AI, HTML for anything with a browser.

Features

  • Your favourite format, instantly. Tick several formats and a quick menu asks which.
  • Right-click the Dock icon for every format.
  • Drop files on the icon or window: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, HTML, RTF, subtitles (.srt, .vtt) and screenshots.
  • Compact for AI: page numbers, padding, timestamps and broken lines go. Fewer tokens, same content. Every file shows roughly how many tokens it is.
  • Reads scans: scanned PDFs and screenshots are read with text recognition, on your Mac.
  • English and Norwegian.

Common Questions

Where do the files go?

To your Downloads folder. Turn on Show in Finder afterwards in TxtMaker's window if you want to see each one.

The shortcut doesn't work

Another app may use ⌃⌥V. Quit that app, or use the menu bar icon. The shortcut can be turned off in TxtMaker's window.

A PDF gives no text

Very low-quality scans may not be readable. Try a clearer scan or a screenshot of the page.

Privacy

TxtMaker collects no data.

Everything you copy or drop is converted on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and TxtMaker never connects to the internet.

TxtMaker does not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Read your clipboard except when you click the icon, press the shortcut or choose Paste
  • Track your usage or use analytics
  • Require an account
  • Share data with third parties

Permissions

Downloads folder: to save the files it makes. Files you choose or drop: to read them. That's all.
